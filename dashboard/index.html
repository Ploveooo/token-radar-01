How the implied value is built

Every row is scored by the same function, from the snapshot's own figures only. No protocol name is ever special-cased. The price is calibrated on the market, not guessed: the snapshot's own live-token protocols — the ones whose token already trades and whose market cap the snapshot records — price each category. A category's multiplier is its live protocols' median market-cap-per-TVL divided by the whole market's median, and a category with fewer than five live protocols to price it falls back to the market median (multiplier 1.00).
